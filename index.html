About Movie:

Raja Shivaji (transl. King Shivaji) is an Indian historical action drama film based on the life of Shivaji, the founder of the Maratha Empire. The film features Riteish Deshmukh in the titular role, alongside Genelia D'Souza, Abhishek Bachchan, Sanjay Dutt, Vidya Balan, Mahesh Manjrekar, Sachin Khedekar, Bhagyashree, Fardeen Khan, Jitendra Joshi and Amole Gupte.
